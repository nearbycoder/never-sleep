Simple by design.

Plug in. Switch on.

Turn on “Keep awake on power” in Never Sleep. Your Mac stays awake whenever it’s connected to external power.

Let the screen rest.

Keep the display awake when you need it, or let it turn off while your work continues.

Always within reach.

Close the window and Never Sleep keeps working in the menu bar. Quit the app to return to normal sleep behavior.

While plugged in, your Mac also stays awake with the lid closed unless you turn that option off. On battery, closing the lid sleeps as usual, and choosing Sleep from the Apple menu always works. Your screen lock settings stay in place.
